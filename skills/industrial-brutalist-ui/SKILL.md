# ⬛ Industrial Brutalist & Hardware Aesthetic Skill

> **Core Philosophy**: *Physical hardware engineering aesthetics. Teenage Engineering and Apple Pro keynote feel with tactile mechanical controls.*

---

## 🛠️ Design Heuristics

1. **Surfaces**: Deep rich charcoal canvas (`#0A0F1D`), dark slate surfaces (`#1E293B`), and subtle paper noise textures.
2. **Accents**: Muted amber (`#F59E0B`), industrial emerald (`#10B981`), or electric blurple (`#7C75FF`). Zero neon purple gradients.
3. **Controls**: Mechanical tactile buttons with distinct 2px borders, visible dot grids, and monospaced telemetry status tags.
