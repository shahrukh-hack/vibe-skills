# ⚪ Minimalist & Swiss UI Design Skill

> **Core Philosophy**: *"Less, but better." — Dieter Rams. Strip away decorative fluff, glows, and noise until only pure functional utility and typographic precision remain.*

---

## 📐 Design Heuristics

1. **Color Restraint**: Maximum 3 colors on screen: High-contrast Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A`), Surgical White (`#FFFFFF`), and Slate 500 (`#64748B`) for secondary metadata.
2. **Typography**: High-tracking monospaced labels (`tracking-widest uppercase`) paired with tightly tracked geometric grotesque headlines (`tracking-[-0.04em]`).
3. **Borders & Dividers**: Hairline `1px` crisp borders (`border-neutral-200` light / `border-neutral-800` dark). Zero drop shadows or glowing outlines.
4. **Information Architecture**: Clean tabular lists, numbered indexes (`01`, `02`, `03`), and keyboard shortcut hints (`⌘K`).
