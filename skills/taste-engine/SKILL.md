# 🪄 Taste Engine — Anti-AI Slop & High-Taste Design Skill

## 🎯 Purpose
Prevents AI agents from generating generic "AI slop" (neon purple glows, pulsing biscuit pills, gradient text keywords, and icon-stuffed bento boxes) by enforcing human-crafted design engineering principles.

## 🚫 Forbidden AI Clichés:
- ❌ **No Purple Gradients on Dark Theme**: Banned. Default to Stripe Enterprise Navy (`#0A2540`), Tailwind UI Slate, or crisp white cards.
- ❌ **No Pulsing Headline Pills**: Banned. Use structured mono badges or editorial kicker lines.
- ❌ **No Gradient Keywords**: Banned. Text should be legible, crisp, and weighted.
- ❌ **No Over-Nested Cards**: Never put 3+ rounded cards inside another rounded card.
- ❌ **No Untracked Large Typefaces**: Large headlines must have tighter letter-spacing (`tracking-tight`).

## ✅ Enforced Design Standards:
1. **Emil Kowalski Physics**: All interactive micro-animations must use spring physics (`stiffness: 420`, `damping: 30`).
2. **Stripe & Tailwind UI Palette**: Use `#F6F9FC` background canvas, `#0A2540` navy headings, `#635BFF` blurple action buttons, and pure white elevated cards with 1px border (`#E3E8EE`).
3. **Tactile Surfaces**: Use subtle border accents, layered depth cards, and active tactile press states (`scale: 0.96`).
