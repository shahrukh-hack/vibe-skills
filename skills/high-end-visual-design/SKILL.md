# 📖 High-End Visual Design & Editorial Skill

> **Core Philosophy**: *Intellectual, timeless, organic, and premium. Design inspired by high-end architectural monographs and Stripe Press.*

---

## 🎨 Design Heuristics

1. **Palette**: Warm cream canvas (`#FAF7F2`), deep espresso ink (`#1A1412`), warm rust/ochre accents (`#C25E38`), and warm almond borders (`#EBE5DF`).
2. **Typography**: Optical size display serif (`Fraunces` / `Playfair`) paired with clean geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
3. **Imagery & Texture**: Subtle paper noise textures (`opacity: 0.035`), asymmetrical editorial layouts, and generous organic whitespace.
